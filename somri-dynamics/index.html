How It Works

Ordering is effortless.

( i )

Talk to us

Reach out on WhatsApp or by call. Tell us exactly what you need — as little or as much detail as you like.

( ii )

We source & verify

We find, inspect and record it. You get real photos and videos of the exact item before any full payment.

( iii )

Pack & ship

Sealed, labelled and tracked. Foodstuff is vacuum-packed for freshness; devices and cars hold verified papers.

( iv )

Confirm & follow up

Delivery confirmed in your hands. We stay on the line for questions, reorders and aftercare.

Verified everything

Documented sourcing, mileage checks on cars, title searches on land, authenticity checks on devices. Proof before payment, always.

Truly worldwide

Levered partnerships with trusted freight and courier networks mean your goods reach Europe, the Americas, Asia and anywhere in Africa.

One human, one line

You talk directly to the team that packs your goods. No call centres, no bots — just straight answers on WhatsApp, email or phone.
